Big the Cat
w/ Egg Walker

*This Character is exclusive to the Dreamcast version.*

Big is a fisherman that lives in the Mystic Ruins with his friend, Froggy. Despite not knowing much about machines, he has shown that he is capable of using them proficiently. Don't underestimate him.
