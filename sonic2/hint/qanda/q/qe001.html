Ｑ：I saved my Chao data to Chao Adventure 2 and removed my VMU from the controller. My Chao data seemed to be broken and when I went back to Chao garden I lost my Chao. How come I have two eggs instead of my Chao? Help me!
Ａ：If you remove the VMU while data is being saved too quickly the data may be corrupted. Check the VMU window to make sure the data is completely saved before removing. The save icon in the window should stop blinking.
