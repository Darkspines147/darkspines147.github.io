"Sonic The Hedgehog #94"
Publication：Archie Comics
Price:$1.99 + tax (CA $2.39 + tax)
Release Date: Released on Febuary 21, 2001

"New Order" In this story, the Freedom Fighters in Knothole must contend with both an earthquake and an internal conflict among the Kintobors.

• Knothole's reconstruction: Six weeks after Prince Elias' coronation, the village of Knothole has been reconstructed with modern amenities. However, Sonic is uneasy, knowing that
Robotnik's presence remains.
• The earthquake: An earthquake suddenly shakes Knothole, waking Sonic and Tails and causing destruction. They rush to save citizens from falling debris.
• The Kintobor family: Meanwhile, Dr. Eggman holds his newest prisoner, Snively, who begs not to be roboticized. Eggman agrees on the condition that Snively pledges allegiance to him.
In return, Eggman promises Snively revenge on his neglectful father, Colin Kintobor.
• School begins: Sonic, Tails, Bunnie, and Rotor attend school, but are separated by age. Sonic, Bunnie, and Mina are taught by Nate Morgan, while Tails is approached by Athair, the
ancient shaman who teleports him away for a greater purpose.
• Sally's education: Sonic learns that Sally Acorn is not in class with them, but is receiving private lessons at Castle Acorn.

"The Best Laid Plans..." This story details a subplot involving Chaos Knuckles and Charmy and Saffron.

• Albionites' experiment: Gala-Na and her Albionite assistants attempt to drain Chaos Knuckles of his immense energy using a Chaos Siphon Suit. A scientist wears the suit to
absorb the power.
• Charmy and Saffron's interference: Charmy Bee and Saffron Bee find Knuckles in distress during the process. Charmy randomly hits the controls, and Saffron reverses a dial
on the siphon suit.
• Knuckles teleports: The reversed energy flow overloads Knuckles with power, causing him to feel a change in his world and teleport away.
• Bounty hunters' refusal: Gala-Na contacts Nic the Weasel and Nack the Weasel for help, but they refuse to work for her again. With no one to stop the now-unstable Chaos
Knuckles, Gala-Na is at a loss.
