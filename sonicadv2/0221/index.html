"Sonic The Hedgehog #94"
Publication：Archie Comics
Price:$1.99 + tax (CA $2.39 + tax)
Release Date: Released on Febuary 21, 2001

Archie Sonic the Hedgehog #94 contains two stories, "New Order" and "The Best Laid Plans...".

"New Order" Six weeks after Prince Elias stripped Sonic of his knighthood, the new Knothole Village is fully constructed with a new castle, hospital, and public school.
Sonic, Tails, Bunnie, and Rotor attend school, where they are grouped by age. Sonic finds himself in a physics class taught by Nate Morgan, and is frustrated
to learn that Sally is taking private lessons at Castle Acorn.Meanwhile, in Robotropolis, Dr. Eggman forces the imprisoned Snively into an alliance.
Snively agrees after Eggman offers him revenge against his neglectful father, Colin Kintobor, who Snively was supposed to kill.
A massive earthquake strikes Knothole, waking Sonic and Tails. While at school, Tails is approached by the shaman Athair, who teleports him away.
During the earthquake, Hope, a young human girl, is frightened by a Robian in the city's underbelly.

"The Best Laid Plans..." Knuckles in trouble: The Albionites, led by Gala-Na, attempt to drain the immense power of Chaos Knuckles using a "Chaos Siphon Suit".
Intervention: Seeking Knuckles, the bees Charmy and Saffron stumble upon the draining process. They attempt to free Knuckles by tampering with the siphon,
but only succeed in overloading him with Chaos energy.
Knuckles vanishes: Overcome with power, Knuckles teleports away from the scene.
The weasels flee: Gala-Na tries to get bounty hunters Nic and Nack to help, but having witnessed the event, the weasels refuse and leave. Gala-Na is left
to deal with the unconscious echidna who received the energy, along with the now-supercharged and missing Knuckles.

"Sonic The Hedgehog #95"
Publication:Archie Comics
Price: $1.99 + tax (CA $2.39 + tax)
Release Date: Released on March 21, 2001

Archie Sonic the Hedgehog #95 contains two stories: "Enemy Mine" and "When Destiny Calls".

Enemy Mine
Believing that the Overlanders have been roboticized, Sonic and Bunnie Rabbot sneak into Robotropolis to free them. They are baffled to find the Overlanders
living as normal civilians and praising Dr. Eggman. Sonic is forced to question what Eggman is truly up to, as the villain's motives are unclear.
The duo is forced to retreat when they find a crazed, radiation-poisoned Kodos the Lion on a rampage in the Great Forest. Kodos is furiously
searching for the Sword of Acorns, but Sonic manages to knock him unconscious.
As Kodos recovers, he ambushes Sonic and ensnares him in a massive spider web. Uma Arachnis, a spider-like being, appears and easily defeats Kodos, allowing
Sonic to escape. She then vanishes without explanation, leaving Sonic even more confused.

When Destiny Calls
Knuckles, struggling to control his Chaos-fueled powers, lashes out and accidentally attacks Tails. Athair, a powerful wizard, teleports Tails to the Floating Island to keep him safe.
Athair reveals that the Ancient Walkers are dying and that Tails's special power is needed to save the Echidna race. Tails is chosen to be the next "Chosen One," a role that Athair believes will save his people.
Knuckles remains on the island, tormented by his uncontrolled powers and the guilt of hurting his friend. He is unaware of the larger destiny that is unfolding around him.

Archie Sonic the Hedgehog #98 is an adaptation of the beginning of the video game Sonic Adventure 2. It primarily sets up the main conflict, introducing Shadow the Hedgehog and establishing the backstory of his creator, Professor Gerald Robotnik.

Sonic Adventure 2
