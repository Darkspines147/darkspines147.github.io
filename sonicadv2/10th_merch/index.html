Rare Sonic Collectibles
Sonic Team has created a selection of limited edition collectibles that are not to be missed. Be one of the few Sonic fans in the world to own these rare mementos of the Blue One's 10th Anniversary.
These are super rare collectables released some time in early 2002. **I couldn't find anymore details as Amazon ZShops is long gone.**
"Sonic 10th Anniversary Figurine"
Manufacturer:Sonic Team?
Price:???
Release Date: 2002

This one-of-a-kind statue is a faithful recreation of Sonic the Hedgehog.
It stands 8.5 inches tall and is the perfect accessory for your desk, coffee table, or anywhere in your home.
The Sonic figure can either stand alone or on it's 10th Anniversary commemorative base.

*Only 250 vere ever made*

"Sonic Crystal Cube"
Manufacturer:Sonic Team?
Price: ???
Release Date: 2002

A three-dimensional, wireframe-style model of Sonic has been laser-etched into a crystal cube.
This is the modern-day equivalent of a ship in a bottle, except its the world's fastest hedgehog preserved in crystal for all eternity!

*Only 500 were ever made*

"Sonic Ring"
Manufacturer:Sonic Team?
Price:???
Release Date:2002

Wear Sonic on your finger to remind you of your favorite Sega superhero.
The limited edition Sonic Ring is made of sterling silver and is slightly adjustable, but be sure to order the correct size.
The ring comes in a black velvet box with a guarantee card indicating the serial number of your particular ring.

*Only 500 were ever made*
