Tiny Chao Garden DX
Developer:Domobot
Operating System Windows, Android
Release Date: TBD

Tiny Chao Garden DX is a fan-made recreation made in GameMaker by domobot, designed to allow you to take your Chao on the go from the PC version of Sonic Adventure 2, just like the GameBoy Advance did for the GameCube
version of Sonic Adventure 2: Battle! You do this by connecting the PC Version of TCGDX to the Android version via local Wi-Fi.

How to Setup Wi-Fi transfer:
  • Go to your Network & Internet Settings, change your Wi-Fi Settings from "Public Network (recommended)" to "Private Network".
  • Enable TCP/UDP Protocols in your Wi-Fi connection.
  • Allow the app through your Firewall settings.
