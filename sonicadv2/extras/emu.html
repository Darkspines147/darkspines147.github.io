Emulators
Emulation is the last resort option of playing older games. If an emulator's name is not highlighted, check the source pages to get a download from the "releases" page. Otherwise download them from the official websites!
This page will NOT share links to BIOS, ROMs or ISOs!! Look for them yourself.
