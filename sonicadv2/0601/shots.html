Screen Shots / Chao World
Everyone, welcome to the "new" world of Chao!
Chao
Chao are back and better than ever!
Now's your chance to raise your very own hero or dark Chao. Imagine your Chao taking on hero traits as they hang out with Sonic, Tails or Knuckles. Better yet, how about raising you Chao to be a little "rascal" being with the likes of Shadow, Dr. Eggman or Rouge. This is all possible with the new breed of Chao in Sonic Adventure 2. Chao are now directly affected by which game characters interact with them, and over time, their personality and appearance will change. Also, your Chao's personality is affected by how you interact with them...bully your Chao and he might just turn out to become a little rascal...
Chao Garden
Welcome to Chao garden that will be your first stop when you acquire the "Chao key" in the action stage. Here you will find Chao eggs, which you have to hatch and then raise. Let's have some fun raising Chao!
This garden is a very quiet and comfortable place where some Chao live. It's very green and even has a pond for Chao to enjoy. This is the entrance to the "Chao Stadium" where the Chao races are held.
Give Chao a small animal and see what happens next! Chao's personality is affected depending on how you raise them!
See Chao watching TV? This is just one of the many rewards that Chao can get when they win races. Try whistling to call your Chao!

Tiny Chao Garden DX
*Exclusive to Sonic Adventure 2 (Steam)*
Tiny Chao Garden DX is a fan-made recreation made in GameMaker by domobot, designed to allow you, in conjunction with Krzys2's Chao Importer/Exporter Tool to take your Chao on the go from the PC version of Sonic Adventure 2, just like the GameBoy Advance did for the GameCube version of Sonic Adventure 2: Battle! You do this by connecting the PC Version of TCGDX to the Android version via local Wi-Fi. This also works with Sonic Adventure DX!
You can now connect "Sonic Adventure 2" for PC with "Tiny Chao Garden DX" You can even transfer your Chao to your Android Phone or Tablet. You can once again take your Chao anywhere you want at anytime. There is a downloadable movie that shows you how to establish the link. To setup the transfer, you need to press the Transporter icon on both Android and PC, and link them up! *Sonic Adventure 2 or Sonic Adventure DX must be running for this to work*
Connect the PC version and Android version of Tiny Chao Garden DX over your local wi-fi connection via TCP/UDP. You will have to allow the game to pass through your firewall settings in order for it to work properly. Preferably over a Private Network. You can also get more rings by playing the mini games or pulling weeds in the garden. Using the Importer/Exporter Tool, you can transfer a Chao to Tiny Chao Garden DX. Highlight a "Empty Slot", click the checkbox next to it, then press either "Export All Selected" to convert that Chao to a .chao file, then tap the stats menu twice to trasfer that chao to Tiny Chao Garden DX, or "Import Chao File" to transfer a Chao back to Sonic Adventure 2.
