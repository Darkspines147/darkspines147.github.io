Sonic Adventure 2 Original Soundtrack Information!
We bring you information on the soundtracks for Sonic Adventure 2 & Sonic Adventure 2: Battle.

This is a list of official music albums made available for purchase for Sonic Adventure 2!
"Cuts Unleashed - SA2 Vocal Collection"
Publication:Marvelous Entertainment
Price:¥2,500 (excluding tax)
Release Date: August 22, 2001
This Vocal Album contains the six main character themes
as well as Escape from the City and
the main theme "Live and Learn".
The album also has hidden instrumentals.

Passion & Pride: Anthems with Attitude
from the Sonic Adventure Era

are two albums including characters' themes from
Sonic Adventure and Sonic Adventure 2. The Vox Collection
contains thirteen tracks and the Instrumental Collection
contains eleven tracks. They are the modern equivilent of this disc.

The Vox collection costs $8.99, the Instrumental collection costs $7.99.

"Multi-Dimensional - Sonic Adventure 2 Original Soundtrack"
Publication:Marvelous Entertainment
Price: ¥3,000 (excluding tax)
Release Date: September 5, 2001

Multi-Dimensional contains all tracks from
Sonic Adventure 2, but like Sonic Adventure,
all the character themes are not included in
this album, since they were featured early on
in the separated vocal album.

Sonic Adventure 2 Original Sound Track

A digital version with two volumes was released in 2014.
Vol. 1 has the tracks from disc 1 and a bonus instrumental
track of "Escape from the City", and
Vol. 2 has the tracks from disc 2 and
a bonus instrumental track of "Live and Learn".
Both volumes cost $9.99.

"Sonic Adventure 2 Official Soundtrack"
Publication:Tokyopop Soundtrax
Price: $14.99 + tax (CA$19.99 + tax)
Release Date: Febuary 5, 2002

A kind of "best of" soundtrack, featuring twenty-six songs.
The modern equivilent is the 20th Anniversary Edition Soundtrack which adds four more songs for an even thirty. It costs $12.99.

"Sonic Adventure 2: Official Soundtrack - Vinyl Edition"
Publication:Brave Wave
Price: $40.00 (¥5,940)
Release Date: December 2017

A special vinyl release!
It was originally available for pre-order on December 5, 2017.
Did you get it?
